# Code of Conduct

NKUGeek Hub is a university technology community. We want discussions,
projects, research collaboration, and competition teams to be rigorous,
welcoming, and safe for students at different experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful in technical disagreements.
- Help newcomers understand context instead of dismissing basic questions.
- Credit authors, maintainers, teammates, datasets, and paper sources.
- Keep private student information private unless explicit consent is given.
- Use issues, reviews, and discussions to improve the project rather than to
  attack people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private contact information, student identity information, or lab
  details without permission.
- Plagiarism, fake contributions, or misrepresenting research and competition
  results.
- Spamming issues, pull requests, forms, or community channels.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to the NKUGeek maintainers through the contact address
listed in `SECURITY.md` or through a private maintainer channel. Maintainers
will review reports with care and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn users, remove content, restrict project access, or ban
accounts when behavior threatens the community or violates this code.
